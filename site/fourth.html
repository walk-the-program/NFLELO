03 / How this works

How this works

What it does. The engine values each fourth-down option by the team's win probability afterward. Going for it uses the play model (run and pass mix by field zone, with conversion offsets); a field goal uses a kicker-aware make model; a punt uses a punter-aware net-yards model. The best option is the one with the highest win probability.

Toss-ups. When the best option beats the second best by less than 0.02 win probability, the call is a toss-up. The cut-off is fixed; there is no uncertainty band, so treat any close call as a coin flip.

Why it is not validated yet. The first version of the conversion model failed its 2020 to 2025 test. The fix was built on development seasons only, frozen before any 2026 game, and will be scored once, after the 2026 participation data is released. Until then these are a model's view, not a validated tool.

What it ignores. Teams know things the model does not: injuries, matchups, weather at the spot, the game plan. Penalties, kneel-downs, ties and overtime are excluded.
