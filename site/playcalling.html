04How this works

How this works

What it does. Teams call plays based on things the data partly captures. The method estimates, for each situation, what the average play would have gained had the team called each candidate action, by correcting for how often teams chose each action (a doubly robust, "AIPW", estimate). An action is a personnel group plus run or pass.

What the gain means. Gain is expected points added per play, compared with the teams' current mix on the same plays. A situation is marked "clear best" only when the best action's interval sits above zero. "No clear best" means the data cannot rank the actions, not that they are equal.

The test. Recommendations for each season come from earlier seasons only. The policy is then scored on the later season by off-policy evaluation. A placebo with shuffled actions should show no gain, and it does not.

The limits. This is an observational estimate. Read the caveats at the top of the page before using any single number.
