04How this works

How this works

What it does. Win probability is the model's estimate of how often a team in a given spot goes on to win: score, time left, down, distance, field position, timeouts and who gets the second-half kickoff, plus how strong the two teams were before the game.

Pregame strength. The pregame input is the A4s game model (Elo, opponent-adjusted efficiency and a quarterback adjustment). No betting line or market number goes in.

The excitement index adds up how far the home win probability moves from snap to snap over the game. A game that swings back and forth scores high; a blowout from the first quarter scores low.

How it was tested. The frozen model was scored once on 2020 to 2025 plays against nflfastR's public model, by Brier score (the average squared miss; lower is better), with the rules written down first.
