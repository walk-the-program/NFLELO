# Elo site

Sub-project context for the static NFLELO website in `site/`. General project context is in `CONTEXT.md`; the model is in `context/data-and-elo.md`.

## Shape

- Static, no build step, no server. Six pages: `site/index.html` (ratings), `site/models.html` (receipts) and the four model pages below (`plays.html`, `winprob.html`, `fourth.html`, `playcalling.html`), sharing five files: `brand.css` (the brand tokens and material classes), `styles.css` (the page styles on top of it), `assets/` (the halftone and glyph grid tiles and the Flow hero background), `common.js` (DOM builders, tooltip, table views, nav highlight, and the one data fetch) and the page script (`app.js` or `models.js`; plain JS, hand-built SVG charts, no CDN scripts), plus `site/data/*.json`. Only external request: Google Fonts (Figtree for headings, Nunito Sans for body). Must be served over http (it fetches `./data/*.json`); `python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory site`, or `nflelo-site` in `.claude/launch.json`.
- Hostable on GitHub Pages by publishing `site/`. The weekly GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/weekly.yml`) runs `scripts/build.py`, `scripts/ml_predict.py` and `scripts/export_site.py`, then commits `experiments/live/`, `site/data/` and `outputs/`.
- Sections, in order: header (wordmark, the matchup instrument, four tiles), power ladder, this week, rest of season (only when `data/ml.json` exists), playoff odds (only when it has a simulation run), team explorer, luck board, league history (tapestry heatmap and parity), records, model scorecard with methodology.

## Exporter (`scripts/export_site.py`)

Reads `outputs/elo_games.csv`, `outputs/ratings_current.json`, `outputs/model_report.md` (legacy Brier only) and the cached `data/raw/schedules.csv`. Writes nine deterministic JSON files (about 0.43 MB total; `history.json` is 0.38 MB of it): `meta`, `ladder`, `upcoming`, `history`, `luck`, `tapestry`, `records`, `scorecard`, `headlines`. `build.py` calls `export()` at the end. Display names, conference and division live in `nflelo/meta.py`.

- **History is not downsampled.** One point per team per regular-season game week since 1970 (about 30k points). Playoff games do not update ratings, so they add nothing. If the file ever matters, drop to season-end points for pre-2000 seasons.
- **Spread sign.** nflverse `spread_line` is positive when the home team is favored (checked: games with `spread_line` above 7 average a home margin near +11). Elo spread is `(home + hfa - away) / 25` with the same sign, so the gap is `elo_spread - vegas_spread`. The site shows both as "FAVORITE -points".
- **This week** is the earliest week of the current season that still has unplayed games, so a Thursday `as_of` shows the rest of that week (the Thursday game is already in the ratings). The three largest absolute Elo-vs-Vegas gaps are flagged. If no lines exist, picks still show and the Vegas fields are null.
- **Headlines (`headlines.json`).** One computed sentence per section, one small function each in the exporter (`headline_ladder`, `headline_week`, `headline_explorer`, `headline_luck`, `headline_history`, `headline_records`, `headline_scorecard`). The explorer has one per team and luck one per season; the page swaps them on selection. Teams are named by city, or by nickname where two teams share the city (New York, Los Angeles; see `nflelo/meta.py`). Edge cases handled: a tie at the top of the ladder, no games left or no Vegas lines in the week, a tie for luckiest team, an unchanged parity year. Explorer lines say "highest/lowest rating since X" only when the rating has gone at least 10 games without being beaten, otherwise distance from average and league rank.
- **Credit and links.** `CREDIT` in the exporter is "Data: nflverse (CC BY 4.0); 1970–1998 results: FiveThirtyEight (CC BY 4.0)." (the wording requested in the licensing round, so it keeps the en dash). The footer shows it through `meta.credit`; the scorecard section has a short Data paragraph and links to nflverse and to FiveThirtyEight's data repository with the required attribution text. Pre-1999 playoff rows exist in `elo_games.csv` but every site table filters to REG, so the JSON outputs did not change.
- **Partial season.** The season in progress is left out of season-end records, best/worst seasons and the scorecard; the tapestry keeps its column and marks it (`partial_season`).
- **Luck** is actual wins minus the sum of pre-game win probabilities, for last season and the current season to date.
- **Scorecard** headline numbers are recomputed from `elo_games.csv` on the held-out 2010-2025 window (Elo 0.2201 Brier, 64.0% accuracy; market 0.2104 and 66.6% on the 4,174 games with moneylines; legacy 0.2244 parsed from the report). Per-season Brier uses games with moneylines (2006 on).

> **Final design (Walker, 2026-10-10): Bento Grid**, per `context/brand.md`. Walker's overrides:
> - **Highlight word:** the one highlight word per page (e.g. "ELO") is the accent `#fbffa0` with an ink text outline (`.hl` in `brand.css`). The `--hl-fill` variable switches it to blue in one line.
> - **Secondary is pure white:** the pink is gone. White chips get a `--line` edge, and white bars and FG swatches get an ink edge.
> - **Patterns:** posterised terrain (`assets/hero-terrain.png`) is the hero on every page, and pebble field (`assets/tex-pebbles.png`) is the one mid-page band per page. The footer is a plain tile. Aura, gradient panes and halftone are retired.
> - **Elo heatmap bins** above average use `--ab-tile-pri`, then `--pri-tint`, then primary.
> - **Archived designs:** `context/archive/`, and tags `design-bento` (v1), `design-ascii`, `design-brutalist-navy`, `design-brutalist`, `design-editorial`.
>
> Older lines below that mention the pink secondary, aura, gradient panes or halftone describe Bento v1 and are superseded by this note.

The current design is **Bento Grid** (Modular, Clear, Product-proud): a board of rounded tiles, each making one point. It is a trial version of the whole site (all six pages), following Walker's brief `context/brand-bento.md` exactly. Same data, scripts and features as before; the markup gained board wrappers, and `brand.css` and `styles.css` were rewritten. Light mode only (no dark theme, no theme toggle, no `localStorage`). Fonts: Figtree 700 for headings and numerals, Nunito Sans for everything else; there is no monospace anywhere.

- **Stylesheets.** `brand.css` holds the brief's variables, the derived tokens, the materials and the control classes (`.b-primary`, `.b-btn`, `.b-seg`, `.b-field`, `.b-switch`, `.b-toggle`, `.b-chip`, `.b-tag`, `.hl`, `.b-bar`, `.b-range`, `.b-meter`, `.tile-m`, `.sub-tile`, `.plate`, `.pat-*`). `styles.css` builds every page from tokens only. No hex colors in `styles.css` or any script; the only hexes in site files are the brief's, in `brand.css`, the two SVGs in `assets/` and the `theme-color` meta (`tests/test_site_pages.py` checks them against the brief's list and fails on the retired navy values).
- **Board and tiles.** `.board` is a grid: 1 column under 600px, 2 from 600px, 4 from 1100px, with 14px gaps. Tiles are `--ab-tile` with radius 28 and one soft shadow, no borders, and hover lifts 2px. Sub-tiles (`--ab-sub`, radius 18) hold fields, controls, inner panels (game facts, the play-console controls, detail panels, table views, play-call cells). Controls are pills. Wide tables stay flat rows inside a tile; there are no per-row tiles.
- **Page shape.** Each page has a hero board, then one board per section: a title tile (`.sec-head`: numbered kicker, h2, deck, and the section's pill controls) and the content tiles. Bodies (`#...-body`) are tiles by default; a body marked `loose` has tiles as children (this week's games, the scorecard tiles, the model cards, the findings, the records tables, the receipts); `tile-stage` is the product-stage tint (`--ab-tile-sec`) on the main interactive tile (matchup instrument, play console, win-probability chart, decision chart, situation grid, and the model scoreboard title tile). `NFL.initSlabs` is a no-op now (no divider strips). The footer is a textured tile plus a credits tile.
- **Hero.** Model pages: a 2x2 pattern tile (terrain) with the title on a white plate, the one primary "01" tile beside it (label bottom-left, 40px arrow circle top-right, on-primary at 18%), a tall lead stat tile (`--sec-tint`) and three stat tiles (huge tabular numerals, tracking -0.05em, small labels). The lead tile carries a secondary progress bar only where the number is a real share: Models (passed of tested), Fourth down (matched share), Win prob (week of 18); `NFL.tileBar` draws it (line track, secondary fill with an ink edge). Ratings page: aura title tile with the wordmark, the primary tile, the lead stat, the matchup instrument as the 3x2 stage, two stats beside it and a wide fourth stat. Models page also uses aura.
- **One primary per view.** Index: "See this week's games". Models: "See the scoreboard". Plays: "Open the play console". Win prob: "Open the game chart". Fourth down: "Open the decision chart". Play calling: "See the test". Everything else primary is a small control state (selected segment, switch on, sort header, nav current page), never a tile. The matchup's "All games this week" link is a ghost link.
- **Highlight and accent.** One `--accent-ink` (`#aa810e`) word per view as large text: ELO in the wordmark, the last word of each model-page title. Accent itself appears only as the footer dot (plus whatever the engine puts in the exact patterns). Never on a tile, button or border.
- **Focus.** 3px primary outline with an outer 1px ink ring (box-shadow), everywhere (fields add the brief's primary edge and 40% ring).
- **Data colors.** Model = `--ink` (solid line, square markers), Elo = `--primary` drawn 3px wide with ink-outlined circle markers, Vegas benchmark = `--muted` dashed with triangles, a third series `--muted` dotted with diamonds (`NFL.mark` draws them; legends and tooltip keys match). Primary (1.80:1) and secondary (1.1:1) are never thin lines or text; they fill bars and areas, and a fill that must be read carries an ink edge (a test enforces the secondary rule). Rest-of-season bars: model projection ink, wins so far muted, Elo tick primary with an ink edge. Fourth-down GO / FG / PUNT = primary / secondary / `--line` fills with an ink outline (clear = solid, lean = tint with ink outline), toss-ups are `--ab-sub` with a dotted ink outline. Play calling: pass = primary, run = secondary, in three steps (sub, tint, solid). Heatmap: seven discrete token bins, pale cells outlined in ink.
- **Patterns (exact, from Walker's engine).** Generated in headless Chromium by loading the engine files in the brief's load order and calling `Patterns.render` (PNG) and `renderSVG` / `tileSVG` (SVG) with the brief's `INKS`, seed `brand` and parameters; nothing is hand-drawn. Assets in `site/assets/`: `hero-aura.png` (2560x1440, about 1.8 MB because of the 0.32 grain), `hero-terrain.png` (2560x1440, 0.6 MB, lossless palette PNG), `tex-pebbles.png` (1280x720, 1.0 MB, defined but not placed yet), `band-gradpanes.svg` (root `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"`) and `tile-halftone.svg` (160px tile). PNGs were optimised losslessly only. Use: aura on the ratings and models heroes, terrain on the four model-page heroes, one gradpanes band per page (a plate with one sentence), halftone on the footer tile (the one textured tile per page). Text always sits on a tile plate, never on a pattern.
- **Layout.** Content up to 1680px with fluid gutters, prose at 70ch, nav is a floating tile that scrolls inside itself when it overflows and never scrolls the page.
- **Fresh data.** Every data fetch goes through `NFL.getJSON` in `common.js` (`cache: 'no-cache'`). Do not add a bare `fetch` for a data file. Optional files resolve to null when missing.
- **Motion.** Ladder and luck bars grow from 1500 once on scroll-in, tile numerals count up, the hero knob slides, tiles lift 2px on hover; all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No em or en dashes in page copy (the credit line from `meta.credit` has an en dash in the data file; `app.js` rewrites digit ranges there to "to" when it renders). No betting language: Vegas is a benchmark only.

## ML model on the site (M4 Phase 1, 2026-10-04)

- `site/data/ml.json` is written by `export_ml` in the exporter only when the ledger `experiments/live/<season>.csv` exists (a stale copy is deleted otherwise), and the nine other files are byte-identical with or without it (tested). The page fetches it optionally; without it nothing changes (the browser logs one 404 for the missing file).
- With it: This week cards get an Elo, Model, Vegas table (home win chance and line); the gap tags and the week headline switch to model vs Vegas in percentage points; a "QB change" line shows when |qb_delta_diff| >= 0.08. JS inserts "Rest of season" after This week, adds its nav link and renumbers the kickers (01 to 08). Projected wins by division (4 columns from 1280px, 2 from 760px): grey wins so far, model bar in `--series-3`, Elo as a tick; each row is a `<details>` with the team's remaining games. The scorecard gets a "Live 2026 record" block and the methodology a Model paragraph with the ledger link and last run time.
- Model color is `--ink` (square marker) and Elo is `--primary` (circle marker); see the design section.

## ML model on the site, Phase 2 (M4, 2026-10-04)

- `ml.json` gains `playoff_odds` (null until `experiments/live/sim_<season>.csv` exists): the latest simulation run per team (playoffs, division, #1 seed, reach and win the Super Bowl, mean wins, 10th and 90th percentile wins), split by conference, with `d_*` changes against a baseline run. The baseline is the latest Wednesday (Eastern) run made more than a day earlier (`live.sim_baseline`), so a Wednesday run compares with last Wednesday and a Sunday run with the Wednesday before it; the deck names the baseline run's time. Each rest-of-season team gets `wins_p10`/`wins_p90`, and each this-week game `spread_model` (from the scored ledger row; null for rows written before the margin model).
- "Playoff odds" section, inserted by JS after Rest of season (or after This week) only when `playoff_odds` exists; `insertSection` adds the nav link and renumbers the kickers. One sortable table per conference (two columns from 1100px): team, playoffs (number plus a `--series-3` bar), change in percentage points (up/down triangle plus sign, `--up`/`--down`, with screen-reader words), division, #1 seed, win Super Bowl. Header buttons sort (`aria-sort`), rows are focusable with a tooltip carrying every number, and a "View playoff odds as a table" details block lists all of them. Team names hide below 1600px (abbreviations stay). Before a baseline exists the change column is left out and the deck says when it will appear.
- Rest of season: a thin `--ink` line under each projection bar marks the 10th to 90th percentile of simulated final wins; tooltip, screen-reader text, legend and table view include it.
- This week: the Model column's line shows the margin model's spread; "Not yet" stays for rows logged before the margin model. Footnote copy adapts.
- Scorecard live block: "Against the Vegas spread" shows the model's record (only rows with a spread) next to Elo's once games are scored; the empty-state copy is unchanged until week 5 is scored.
- Scorecard live block, shadow line (2026-10-05): one `fnote` paragraph after the notes, "Shadow model (Kalman, testing for 2027): Brier X against the model's Y on the same N games. It does not change the model's picks. See its ledger." From `ml.json` `shadow` (the M3b C2d shadow, scored on the same games as the model; see `context/ml.md`, "Shadow deployment"). Hidden when `shadow` is null or has no scored games, so it can only appear once the live record has games. Checked light and dark at 390 and 1440 px: same type and color as the other notes, no overflow, no console errors.
- Methodology: a "Playoff odds" paragraph (margin model, key numbers, the simulation with season-long shocks, the tiebreaker check, and the DEV calibration caveat in plain words).
- Without `ml.json`, or with an `ml.json` that has no simulation, the page is unchanged (tested in `tests/ml/test_live.py`).
- **Gate (2026-10-04, Walker):** the playoff odds and the win range stay off the site until the M4 holdout sign-off. `experiments/live/publish.json` (committed) holds `"publish_sim": false`; the exporter then writes `playoff_odds: null`, `sim_published: false` and null win ranges, even though the weekly job keeps simulating and appending to `sim_<season>.csv`. A missing file also means off. Flip it to true by hand after sign-off (and update `test_committed_publish_flag_keeps_the_simulation_off_the_site`). Margin spreads are not gated.

## Model pages (added 2026-10-10)

Four pages show the tested models: `plays.html` (M6 play outcomes), `winprob.html` (M7a win probability), `fourth.html` (M7a-v2 fourth downs), `playcalling.html` (M7b early-down play calling). Each has one HTML and one JS (`plays.js`, `winprob.js`, `fourth.js`, `playcalling.js`), reads one file written by `scripts/export_ml_pages.py`, and follows the data contract in `context/ml-pages.md` (field names, headline numbers, and the caveats that must appear). Shared helpers live in `common.js` (`seg`, `heroTiles`, `ciChart`, `tableViewLive`, `caveatBox`, `unavailable`, `licenseUI`, `receiptLinks`, formatting and field-position labels). The HTML is generated once from a template and then edited by hand like the other pages; there is no build step.

- **Shared shape.** Glass hero with a computed headline (built from the JSON numbers, never typed) and four flat tiles, the license chip (CC BY or CC BY-SA, from `license`) in the kicker row and the credit line in the footer, numbered sections with data-driven h2 headlines, then "How this works" (plain-English method, links to the method doc on GitHub, the run record and commit, the caveats, definitions). Every data fetch goes through `NFL.getJSON` with `no-cache`. A missing file gives "Data not available yet" in the hero and each section (tested with a mocked 404). No betting language; Vegas does not appear on these pages at all.
- **Nav.** Every page now has a "Models" group after a divider: Plays, Win prob, Fourth down, Play calling, Receipts (`models.html`). On the new pages the current link has `aria-current="page"` (styled like the section highlight). The nav scrolls sideways inside the glass bar at 390px; the page does not (checked at 390, 1440, 2000).
- **Plays.** Glass "play console": segmented down (1-4), distance (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15), call (run, pass) and personnel (11, 12, 21, 13, with the group's makeup below), and a recessed single-thumb slider over the eight yard-line values (own 5 to opp 5). Distances longer than the field position are dimmed and snap down. Output: the yard distribution as violet bars (10 or more lost to 41+), a dashed line to gain with a shaded "first down or TD" region, the TD as its own bar, and the turnover (a separate model that overlaps the others) as an outline bar; big numerals for expected yards, first down or TD, 20+, yards lost and turnover. "Thin support: few similar real plays" shows when `n_similar` is under 30 (a stronger sentence at 0). Pin a setting to compare: the pinned distribution is a stepped outline and each numeral shows the pinned value and the difference. Tooltip and arrow keys read each bar; table view lists every result. The held-neutral inputs are printed under the console and again in "How this works". The receipt is an interval chart of the CRPS difference for the call and situation views.
- **Win prob.** Game picker (grouped by week) with previous and next, a large line chart of home win probability (50 percent line, quarter marks, halftime rule, biggest swing ringed and annotated, crosshair tooltip, arrow keys with Shift for ten snaps, S jumps to the swing), the swing play text, five facts, an overtime note, and a table view. The play text exists only for the biggest swing (the contract has no per-snap text), so the tooltip shows it at that point. Excitement leaderboard: flat rows sorted by excitement, biggest swing or the winner's low point (header buttons; a segmented control on narrow screens), top 10 with "Show all", a row opens its game in the chart. Held-back games are listed under the table. Receipts: ours against nflfastR (Brier, difference with its interval, calibration error).
- **Fourth.** A visible status notice first (the contract's `status.plain` verbatim, frozen commit, earliest dates, and a passed, failed or pending chip per component). Decision chart: heatmap of yard line by distance (945 squares) in three categorical hues, GO (teal), FG (gold), PUNT (rose), `--cat-go`, `--cat-fg`, `--cat-punt`, chosen away from the Elo, model and Vegas colors and validated with the dataviz script (all pairs, dark and light canvases: CVD delta E at least 10.4, normal vision at least 17.7, contrast at least 3:1). Margin sets the intensity, so close calls are faint; a switch adds a dot on toss-ups. Preset segmented control (tied Q2, down 4 early Q4, up 3 late Q4), tooltip and arrow keys, a detail line for touch, and a table of all squares; the label "Point estimates without the uncertainty band" is above the chart. On narrow screens the chart keeps a 600px minimum and scrolls inside its well. "2026 so far": counts, choices against the model's preference as stacked bars, a sortable team table (the team name filters the list), and a filterable plays list (team select; all, differed, toss-ups) with expandable rows. Every figure carries "model-estimated" (hero tiles, chips on the section titles, table captions, tooltips). The section id is `ytd`, not `live`, because `#live` is the shared aria-live region.
- **Play calling.** Caveats first, in a recessed box (unmeasured confounding, defenses adapt, leftover bias, "no clear best" is not "equal"). "The test": interval charts for the gain, the placebo and the two stress tests, and per season, with the success-rate gain, the share of plays affected and the plays tested; the season note is computed (2022's gain interval includes zero and its placebo does not). "Situation grid": five down-and-distance rows by four field zones, a segmented score state and season; clear cells are tinted by action (pass teal, run gold, reusing GO and FG hues) with the gain, "no clear best" cells are dashed and neutral, click or focus for the ranked actions, table view of all 60 situations. "Team view, 2025": flat dot-plot rows against the league tick, labelled descriptive, sortable by pass rate or name, with the pass-recommended situations in a table view.
- **Upkeep.** If the exporter changes a field, `context/ml-pages.md` changes first and then these scripts. `tests/test_site_pages.py` checks that the nav links are on every page, local assets exist, every id a script looks up exists in its HTML, `#live` is not reused, and that the page copy has no dashes or betting words.
